(conductor-write.js:3-14) is: a spoken utterance is transcribed and written into the conductor
TUI input; without a supervisor-enforced boundary the CLI could process it as a full agent turn
with tool access. The four guards ensure every turn is ARMED by a live supervisor process,
CONFIRMED by a vendor hook dispatching back to that supervisor, and DENIED if either is missing.

For typed operator text: threats 2 (residue) and 3 (pane not accepting text) apply equally.
Threat 1 (CLI self-authorizing tools) applies DIFFERENTLY because the operator IS the authorizing
party for typed text. The code makes no distinction between spoken and typed input.
