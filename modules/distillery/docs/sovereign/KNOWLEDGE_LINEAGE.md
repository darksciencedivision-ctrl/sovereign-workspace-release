# Distillery Knowledge Lineage

The Distillery Knowledge Lineage is a structured experiment database, not a learned oracle.
Its accepted ownership and scope are recorded in [`DECISIONS-v1.md`](DECISIONS-v1.md) and
[`INTEGRATION_REVIEW.md`](INTEGRATION_REVIEW.md).

It records, at minimum:

- attempt and run identity;
- teacher/student pair and immutable artifact identities;
- architecture, curriculum, dataset, and hyperparameters;
- hardware, runtime, VRAM/RAM, and timing;
- useful deltas and `SKIPPED_NO_DELTA` decisions;
- gains, regressions, failures, and remediation;
- M/T/D, criticality, governed-floor, and human promotion evidence;
- quantization effects and deployment-artifact lineage;
- seed choice and every license inherited by the persistent Sovereign model lineage.

The Distillery owns this lineage. Sovereign and sibling systems may consume or synchronize
it as evidence; they do not silently redefine or own it. This preserves standalone operation
and the native Sovereign trajectory.
